# Business Analysis

## Purpose

This section documents the business discovery process performed before implementing the solution.

The objective is to understand how the business operates, identify the core concepts, define the ubiquitous language, and capture the business rules that drive the domain model.

The documents contained in this folder describe the business independently of any technical implementation.

## Objectives

The business analysis phase aims to:

- Understand the business domain.
- Identify the ubiquitous language.
- Discover business concepts.
- Capture business rules.
- Identify business invariants.
- Define aggregate boundaries.
- Reduce uncertainty before implementation.
